About Me

I am a final-year Information Technology student at Stamford International University (3.96 GPA, Full Scholarship Recipient), specializing in Data Science. My work focuses on turning raw, messy data into structured, decision-ready insight. This includes predicting outcomes and trends, designing clean database schemas, building business-ready Power BI dashboards, and, more recently, preparing and annotating datasets used to train AI models as a freelance Data Annotation and Preparation Specialist.

As Vice President of the TechBridge IT Club, I lead a cross-functional student team, coordinate technical workshops, and manage the club's digital media output from planning to execution. This combination of technical depth in data, practical understanding of how systems are built, and experience leading people allows me to translate complex technical work into clear outcomes for both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
